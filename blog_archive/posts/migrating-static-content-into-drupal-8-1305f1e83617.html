Migrating static content into Drupal 8

Strategies and tools for moving mountains of unstructured government content

CivicActions · 2019-12-12T23:00:04.162000Z

Original on Medium

At DrupalCon 2019, CivicActions engineer Kristian Ducharme offered hope to those dealing with large, messy migrations projects that have a lot of unstructured content. Kristian is a veteran content shepherd, and enjoys helping government agencies strategically modernize their sites to make life better for content managers and users.

In this talk, Kristian shows us how to use the Migration Tools module developed by CivicActions to find structure in your existing content to make migration smooth and efficient. See Migration Tools in action with a use case that tackles 15 years of government static content for migration into a new Drupal 8 system.
