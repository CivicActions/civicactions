Single Sign On with Drupal

At DrupalGovCon 2019, CivicActions engineer Dan Gurin led a session on the sometimes tricky process of implementing Single Sign On (SSO) in Drupal. Dan loves building Drupal sites to meet the specialized needs of government clients, including making user management simple and secure.

In this session, Dan discusses use cases and gives advice on key questions that can help improve collaboration with government clients. He also talks through the practicals — such as how to install and configure SimpleSAML Service and Identity Providers, and how to leverage MAX.gov to further integrate SSO into existing government systems.
